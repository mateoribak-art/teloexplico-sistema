🚗 You warm up your car every winter morning. The Department of Energy says that's backwards.

Idling gets zero miles per gallon, and the engine warms up faster when you drive it. Fueleconomy.gov says in winter, start driving gently after about thirty seconds.

Cold hurts your mileage anyway: in city tests, gas cars got roughly 15% fewer miles per gallon at 20°F than at 77°F.

Heads up: that's what most manufacturers recommend, so check your owner's manual.
