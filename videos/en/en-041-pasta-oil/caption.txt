Oil in your pasta water won't stop it from sticking 🍝

America's Test Kitchen says oil in the cooking water does not help pasta from sticking to itself. What does: a hard boil and stirring, especially at the start of cooking.

Heads up: this is only about sticking in the pot. Oil is still fine in a sauce or a skillet.

📌 Save this: no oil, hard boil, stir at the start.
