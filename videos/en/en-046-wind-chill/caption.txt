🥶 Your weather app says it feels like 19 below. Your pipes and your car don't feel that.

The National Weather Service says wind chill is how cold it feels to people and animals. Wind cools objects faster, but never below the actual air temperature.

At 0°F with a 15 mph wind, the wind chill is -19°F, and exposed skin can freeze in 30 minutes.

Heads up: for you, that feels-like number is real. Cover up your skin.
