DESCRIPCIÓN:
Sugar makes kids hyper? That's a myth 🍬

Researchers pooled double-blind studies, where nobody knew who got real sugar and who got a fake. A big review in JAMA (1995) found no effect on kids' behavior.

In one study, moms were told their sons had sugar and rated them as more hyper. Every kid had actually gotten a sugar-free placebo.

Heads up: this is about behavior. Candy can still be rough on teeth.
