Thanksgiving turkey still frozen? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Thanksgiving turkey still frozen? Not on the counter. 🦃🧊

The USDA says the safest way to thaw it is in the fridge: about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds. A 16-pound bird needs about 3 to 4 days.

Running late? Cold water works: keep it in its wrapper, allow about 30 minutes per pound, and change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right after.

Heads up: the USDA also says you can cook a turkey from frozen. It just takes about 50% longer.
