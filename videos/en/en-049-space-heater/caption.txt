🔥 That space heater is plugged into a power strip. Don't.

The Consumer Product Safety Commission says not to use extension cords or power strips with space heaters. The U.S. Fire Administration says to plug them directly into a wall outlet.

Keep it at least 3 feet from curtains, beds, sofas, clothes and papers.

Heads up: never leave it on while it's unattended, or while you're sleeping.

💾 Save this: wall outlet only, 3 feet of space, off when you sleep.
