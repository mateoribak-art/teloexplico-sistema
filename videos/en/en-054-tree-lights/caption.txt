Your tree lights stay on all night? Turn them off. 🎄🔥

NIST tested two Christmas trees: the dry one caught fire, the watered one didn't ignite. NIST's advice: keep the tree well-watered and check the water level in the stand every day, and always turn off tree lights before leaving home or going to bed.

Heads up: only use decorative lights with the label of a recognized testing laboratory.
