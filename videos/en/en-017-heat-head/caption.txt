You don't lose most of your body heat through your head 🧢❌ That's a myth.

Heat escapes from any bare skin, roughly in proportion to how much is showing. Your head is only about 10% of your body's surface, so it loses roughly 10% of your heat at most, not half.

Doctors writing in the BMJ think the myth probably started with an old military test where volunteers wore arctic suits but no hats. That would explain why their heads lost the most: they were the only skin showing.

Heads up: a bare head in the cold still loses heat, so a hat helps. It just isn't special. Cover whatever is bare.
