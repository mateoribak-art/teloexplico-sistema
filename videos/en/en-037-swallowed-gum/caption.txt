Swallowed gum stays in your stomach for seven years? Nope, that's a myth 🍬

Gum base can't be broken down by digestion, so it passes through whole. Cleveland Clinic says swallowed gum comes out whole, about 40 hours later.

Heads up: Cleveland Clinic warns that swallowing lots of gum, or doing it every day, can cause a blockage. KidsHealth says blockages from gum are rare. If you get belly pain or vomiting after swallowing a lot, call a doctor.
