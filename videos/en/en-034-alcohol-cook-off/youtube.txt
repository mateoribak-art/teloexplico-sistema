DESCRIPCIÓN:
You think the alcohol cooked off the second the wine hit the pan. It didn't 🍷

The USDA's retention table says a dish simmered for 15 minutes, with the alcohol stirred in, still holds about 40% of the alcohol. After 1 hour: about 25%. After 2.5 hours: about 5%. If you flambé, the table says about 75%.

📌 Save this: 15 min = 40% · 1 hour = 25% · 2.5 hours = 5%

Heads up: these are estimates, and they change with the pan, the stirring and the recipe. If someone at your table avoids alcohol completely, leave the wine out.
