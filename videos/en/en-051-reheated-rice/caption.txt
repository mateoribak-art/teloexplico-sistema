That rice you left out all afternoon? Reheating it tonight doesn't fix it. 🍚

Raw rice can carry bacterial spores that survive cooking. If cooked rice sits out, the bacteria can multiply and make a toxin that reheating may not destroy. A CDC report traced an outbreak to fried rice held that way.

The USDA says to refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours (1 hour if it's over 90°F), eat them within 3 to 4 days, and reheat to 165°F.

📌 Save this: 2 hours · 3-4 days · 165°F.

Heads up: reheating isn't the problem. The time the rice spent sitting out is.
