Generator in the garage with the door open? That's not safe. ⚠️

The Consumer Product Safety Commission says opening doors or windows won't give you enough ventilation to stop carbon monoxide from building up to lethal levels. You can't see it or smell it.

The rule: run a portable generator outside only, at least 20 feet away from the house. Not on the porch, not in the carport.
