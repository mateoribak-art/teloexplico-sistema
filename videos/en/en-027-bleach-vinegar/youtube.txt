Bleach  vinegar so it cleans better? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Bleach + vinegar so it cleans better? What you actually make is a gas. 🧪🚫

Vinegar is an acid, and the CDC and Poison Control say bleach mixed with an acid releases chlorine gas. With cleaners that contain ammonia, bleach forms chloramines, another irritating gas. Chlorine gas can burn the eyes, nose and throat, and cause coughing.

The CDC's rule is simple: never mix bleach with ammonia or any other cleaner.

Heads up: the gas comes from the mixing. Use bleach alone, follow the label, and keep the air moving.
