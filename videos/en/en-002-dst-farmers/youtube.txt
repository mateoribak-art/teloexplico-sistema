DESCRIPCIÓN:
🕐 Daylight saving time was NOT made for farmers. It's a myth.

Germany was the first big country to try it, in 1916, to save fuel during World War I. The U.S. followed in 1918 for the same war reason. Farmers hated it, and their push helped get Congress to repeal it in 1919.

Heads up: it came back later, and a federal law (the Uniform Time Act) has set the dates since 1966.
