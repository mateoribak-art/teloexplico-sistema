DESCRIPCIÓN:
You don't need a huge crowd for a shared birthday. Twenty-three people is enough 🎂

With 23 people, the odds that at least two share a birthday are about 50.7%. The trick is pairs: 23 people make 253 different pairs, and each one is a chance to match. At 40 people it's about 89%, and at 70 it's over 99%.

Heads up: the math assumes all 365 days are equally likely. Real birthdays aren't evenly spread, which makes a match slightly more likely, not less.
