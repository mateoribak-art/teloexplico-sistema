💧 What gives you a cold is a virus. Wet hair? It's not on the CDC's list.

The CDC says more than two hundred viruses can cause a cold.

In 2005, researchers at Cardiff University chilled the feet of ninety volunteers: thirteen said they had a cold a few days later. In the group of ninety who weren't chilled, five.

Heads up: they chilled feet, not hair, it was a small study, and it didn't measure viruses. So it doesn't show that wet hair gives you a cold, and it doesn't settle whether chilling matters.

Cold viruses spread through droplets and surfaces, and the CDC says washing your hands helps lower your risk.
