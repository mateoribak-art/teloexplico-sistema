Your eggs belong in the fridge, not in that cute bowl on the counter 🥚

In the U.S., producers wash and refrigerate their eggs (USDA Agricultural Research Service). The shell's thin natural coating, the cuticle, dries and comes off, and a USDA study found egg quality stays high in the fridge and drops fast without it.

The FDA says: keep eggs at 40°F or below, in the original carton, and use them within 3 weeks for best quality.

Heads up: this is about eggs sold in the U.S. Other countries handle their eggs differently.
