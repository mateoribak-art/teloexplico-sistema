That package problem text? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
That "package problem" text? Don't tap the link. 📦🚫

The FTC says these fake texts claim unpaid postage, a missed delivery, or a shipping update. The link leads to a look-alike of a real website that asks for your personal or financial information.

Heads up: a real package might be on its way. Just don't use the link in the text. Check tracking through the store or account where you ordered, then report the text in your messaging app or forward it to 7726.
