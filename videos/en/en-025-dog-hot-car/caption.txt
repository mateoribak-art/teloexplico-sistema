"Just a minute" in a hot car with the window cracked? It's not as safe as it feels. 🐕🚗

A Pediatrics study (2005) tracked a dark car in the sun for one hour. On days with highs from 72 to 96°F, the inside rose about 40°F, and about 80% of that rise happened in the first 30 minutes. Cracked windows did not significantly slow it down.

Heads up: that was one dark car on sunny days. But a dog locked inside can't leave, and can get heatstroke. The AVMA says never leave a pet alone in a parked car.
