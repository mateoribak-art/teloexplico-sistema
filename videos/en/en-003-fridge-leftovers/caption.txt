Letting leftovers cool on the counter first? That's backwards. 🥘

The USDA says hot food can go straight into the fridge. On the counter, it can sit in the "Danger Zone" (40°F to 140°F), where bacteria can double in as little as 20 minutes.

The myth likely comes from old iceboxes, where hot food melted the ice. Modern fridges handle it fine.

Heads up: a giant pot takes forever to cool, even inside. Split it into shallow containers. The rule: refrigerate within 2 hours, or within 1 hour if it's above 90°F out.
