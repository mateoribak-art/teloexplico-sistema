Salt on your icy steps and nothing happens? It can be too cold for it. 🧊🧂

Rock salt is effective down to about 15°F. Below that it works very slowly and won't melt much ice, and below about -6°F it stops working altogether. And that's the pavement temperature, not the air temperature.

Heads up: use deicer selectively and sparingly.
