Stop typing the tip into your phone 🧮 There's a two-step shortcut.

Say the bill is $50. Slide the decimal one spot left: that's 10%, so $5. Double it: $10 is 20%.

📌 Save this: 10% = slide the decimal. 20% = double it. 15% = 10% plus half of it ($5 + $2.50 = $7.50).

Heads up: some restaurants already add a service charge to the bill, so check it before you tip.
