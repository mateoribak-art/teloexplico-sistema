Your phone dying at 40% in the cold? The cold can do that 🥶

Apple says iPhones are designed for temperatures between 32 and 95 °F. In very cold conditions, battery life can get temporarily shorter and the phone could shut itself off. It might also stop charging.

Apple says battery life returns to normal when the phone is back at higher temperatures.

Heads up: Apple also says not to leave your phone in a parked car, where the temperature can go outside that range.
