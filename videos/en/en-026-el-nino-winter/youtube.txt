DESCRIPCIÓN:
Your winter is being shaped by the Pacific Ocean. Meet El Niño. 🌊❄️

NOAA's Climate Prediction Center says El Niño is back: the central Pacific along the equator is running about 2.1°C (roughly 4°F) above normal, and a strong El Niño is likely to last through the winter, into March.

That warm water tends to shift the storm track, so the South usually ends up wetter and the northern states usually end up warmer.

Heads up: that's the odds, not a promise. Your own town can still get a blizzard. Check your local forecast at weather.gov.
