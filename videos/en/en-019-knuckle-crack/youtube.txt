Cracking your knuckles gives you arthritis? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Cracking your knuckles gives you arthritis? Studies haven't found that 🤜

In a 2011 study, researchers surveyed 215 adults ages 50 to 89. People who cracked their knuckles had no more hand arthritis than people who didn't.

And the pop? A real-time MRI study from the University of Alberta filmed it: a dark gap opens up in the joint as the sound happens. The researchers think it's a bubble of gas.

Heads up: those are small studies, and a perfect one is almost impossible to run. But so far, the pop alone isn't a reason to panic.
