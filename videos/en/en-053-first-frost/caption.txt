Forecast says 36°F tonight? Frost can still hit your plants 🥶

Many National Weather Service offices issue a frost advisory when lows are forecast between 33 and 36°F on clear, calm nights. The air near the ground can be colder than the thermometer reading, so frost can form even when the forecast is above freezing.

Garden experts say to cover tender plants with a bedsheet before sunset, or bring them inside.

Heads up: hardy, established plants usually handle frost fine. It's the tender ones at risk.
