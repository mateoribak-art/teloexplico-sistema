The smoke alarm on your ceiling might be expired. ⏰🔥

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) says to replace smoke alarms at least every 10 years. The 10 years count from the manufacture date printed on the alarm, not from the day you bought or installed it. NFPA also recommends testing your alarms once a month.

Heads up: a chirp usually means the battery is low, so swap it. If it keeps chirping, or it's a sealed 10-year alarm, replace the whole alarm.
