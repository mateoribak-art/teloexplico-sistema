If you've ever been to Miami, GTA 6 is going to look familiar 🌴🎮

Vice City, the game's city, is inspired by Miami according to the press and Rockstar interviews, and the state of Leonida is inspired by Florida according to media reports. Reporters in Miami compared the trailer to the real city: according to Miami New Times, the Venetian Causeway, the bridge linking Miami and Miami Beach, shows up recreated in the game.

Heads up: as far as we know, Rockstar hasn't confirmed which specific places are real; these are trailer comparisons. Its official site calls Vice City "the darkest side of the sunniest place in America," without naming Miami.
