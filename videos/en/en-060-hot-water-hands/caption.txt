Hot water doesn't wash your hands cleaner 🧼

The CDC says the temperature of the water doesn't change how many germs come off. A 2017 Rutgers study tested water from 60 to 100 °F and found no meaningful difference in germ removal.

What does the work: soap and scrubbing. Wet, soap, scrub for 20 seconds, rinse, dry.

Heads up: hotter water can irritate your skin, so use whatever feels comfortable.
