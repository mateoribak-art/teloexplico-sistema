📱 Wet phone? Don't put it in rice: Apple says no.

The idea behind the trick is that rice soaks up the moisture. But Apple's support page says it word for word: "Don't put your iPhone in a bag of rice." The reason: small particles of rice could damage your iPhone.

What Apple says to do instead: unplug it, tap it gently against your hand with the connector facing down, leave it in a dry area with some airflow (no heat like a hair dryer, no compressed air) and wait at least 30 minutes before charging. If the alert comes back, it can take up to a day to fully dry.

Heads up: this is Apple's advice for iPhone. For other brands, check what your manufacturer says.
