Searing doesn't seal in the juices 🥩 That's a myth.

A seared crust doesn't seal anything: steaks lose juice as they cook, seared or not. America's Test Kitchen tested it with rib-eyes, and steaks lost about the same amount of juice whether they were seared first or last.

So why sear? Browning adds flavor and a crust.

Heads up: searing is still worth it. It just isn't a seal.
