"It's too cold to snow." That's a myth ❄️

NOAA's National Severe Storms Laboratory says it rarely snows when the temperature drops below zero degrees Fahrenheit, but because the atmosphere is too stable, not because it's too cold. "Too cold to snow" really means there isn't enough lifting of air to bring snow down to the surface. And even at very cold surface temperatures, significant snowfall can occur.

Heads up: below zero, snow really is rare. It's just not a temperature limit.
