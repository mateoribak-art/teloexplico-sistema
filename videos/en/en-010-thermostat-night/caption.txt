Turning the heat down at night can save money 🌙🔥 Reheating the house doesn't cancel it out.

A house loses heat faster when the gap between inside and outside is bigger. A cooler house leaks less heat all night long.

The U.S. Department of Energy says turning your thermostat back 7 to 10 degrees F for 8 hours a day can save as much as 10% a year on heating and cooling.

Heads up: that's an upper estimate, and heat pumps are different, so check yours first.
