DESCRIPCIÓN:
The five-second rule? It's a myth 🍪

Researchers at Rutgers University dropped watermelon, bread, buttered bread, and gummy candy on stainless steel, tile, wood, and carpet. In some cases, bacteria got on the food in less than one second. Wetter food picked up more (watermelon the most, gummy candy the least), and carpet passed on much less than steel or tile.

Heads up: longer contact does mean more bacteria. Time just isn't the whole story. When in doubt, toss it.
