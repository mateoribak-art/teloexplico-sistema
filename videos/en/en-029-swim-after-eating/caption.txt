Wait thirty minutes before swimming after lunch? The evidence says you don't have to. 🏊

Researchers haven't found any "digestion cramp" that makes swimmers sink. The American Red Cross reviewed the research and found no evidence that swimming after eating causes drowning. One early printed warning is often traced to a Boy Scouts handbook from 1908.

Heads up: after a huge meal you might get a side stitch. Annoying, but nothing the research ties to drowning. What does matter is alcohol: the CDC links it to a large share of teen and adult deaths around water.
