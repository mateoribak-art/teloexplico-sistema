Count the seconds between the flash and the thunder ⚡ That's how far away the lightning was.

Light reaches you almost instantly, but sound is much slower. The rule of thumb: about 5 seconds per mile. Count 15 seconds and the lightning was roughly 3 miles away.

Heads up: it's an estimate, not an exact measurement. And if you can hear thunder, the NWS says you're likely within striking distance of the storm. When thunder roars, go indoors.
