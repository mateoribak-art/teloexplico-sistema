Setting the AC to 60 won't cool your house faster. 🥶❌

A thermostat isn't a gas pedal, it's a destination. A basic AC runs at one speed, so asking for 60 won't speed it up. It just keeps running past the temperature you wanted.

The U.S. Department of Energy says setting the thermostat colder than normal won't cool your home any faster, and could mean excessive cooling and unnecessary expense.

Heads up: with an open window or the sun pouring in, no number gets you there. Close the blinds first.
