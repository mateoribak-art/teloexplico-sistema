Setting the AC to 60 won't cool your house faster. 🥶❌

A thermostat isn't a gas pedal, it's a destination. A basic AC runs at full capacity whenever the thermostat calls for cooling, then shuts off once it hits your temperature. A lower number only changes where it stops, not how fast it gets there.

Heads up: with an open window or the sun pouring in, no number gets you there. Close the blinds first.
