Rinsing your raw chicken? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Rinsing your raw chicken? That's backwards 🐔

Water doesn't kill the bacteria on chicken, and the CDC says rinsing can spread germs to your sink, your counter, and other food. In a USDA-backed study with about 300 people in test kitchens, 60% of those who washed their chicken had bacteria in the sink afterward.

What kills bacteria is cooking: chicken needs to reach 165°F inside.

Heads up: do wash your hands with soap and water after touching raw chicken.
