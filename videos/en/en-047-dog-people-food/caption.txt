🐶 You slip your dog a bite off your plate. Some of it can really hurt them.

The ASPCA keeps a list of people foods to avoid feeding pets. Three on it: chocolate (the darker, the bigger the risk), grapes and raisins (can cause kidney damage), and onions, garlic and chives (can damage red blood cells and lead to anemia).

Heads up: the risk depends on how much your dog ate. If they got into any of these, call your vet.

💾 Save this: never for dogs — chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions, garlic, chives.
