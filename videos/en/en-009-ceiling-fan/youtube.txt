DESCRIPCIÓN:
Most ceiling fans have a direction switch, and you've probably never touched yours 🌀

In summer, the blades should spin counterclockwise (as you look up at them), pushing air down so you feel a cooling breeze. In winter, switch to clockwise on low: the fan pulls air up and pushes the warm air near the ceiling down and around the room.

That's the advice from the U.S. Department of Energy.

Heads up: a fan cools people, not rooms, so turn it off when you leave.
