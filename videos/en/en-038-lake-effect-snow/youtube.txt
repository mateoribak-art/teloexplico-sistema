DESCRIPCIÓN:
Buried in snow while the next town is sunny? That's lake-effect snow ❄️

Cold air, often from Canada, moves across the open waters of the Great Lakes. The lake adds heat and moisture to the lowest layer of air, it rises, and the clouds merge into a narrow band.

The National Weather Service says one band can produce 2 to 3 inches of snow per hour or more.

Heads up: bands are narrow, so snow can fall in one spot while it's sunny just a mile or two away.
