Thawed too much chicken? You can usually freeze it again ❄️ It depends on how you thawed it.

The USDA says food thawed in the refrigerator is safe to refreeze without cooking. Food thawed in cold water or the microwave should be cooked before you refreeze it. And food left out of the refrigerator for more than two hours shouldn't be refrozen.

Heads up: refrozen meat can come out a little drier, because it loses moisture as it thaws.

📌 Save this: fridge thaw = refreeze raw. Cold water or microwave = cook first. Out over 2 hours = toss it.
