"Polar vortex" on the news? It's not a storm headed for your town 🥶

It's a large area of cold air and low pressure circling each pole all year. It strengthens in winter, and when it expands, cold air can get sent south on the jet stream.

The National Weather Service says that's often tied to outbreaks of Arctic air in the U.S.

Heads up: it's nothing new, and the real danger is how cold it gets.
