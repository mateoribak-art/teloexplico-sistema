Sleepy after Thanksgiving dinner? Don't blame the turkey. 🦃😴

A nursing professor at UNC Charlotte says turkey has no meaningfully more tryptophan than chicken, beef, nuts, or cheese. He says the main cause of the post-dinner slump is the large amount of carbs: stuffing, mashed potatoes, rolls, and desserts. Overeating and alcohol contribute too.

Heads up: experts still debate how much each of these matters, so there's no single magic culprit. Try a walk after dinner before you blame the bird.
