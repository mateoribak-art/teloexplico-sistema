GTA 6 was set for November 19, 2026, and GTA V came out 13 years ago 🎮⏳

GTA V launched on September 17, 2013. Rockstar set GTA 6's release for November 19, 2026: 13 years and 2 months apart.

And it was supposed to be sooner. Rockstar announced it for 2025, then moved it to May 26, 2026 (announced May 2025), and then to November 19, 2026 (announced November 2025). Two delays.
