Thawing meat on the counter? That's backwards 🥩

The outside of the meat warms up while the middle can still be frozen. That outer layer can end up between 40 °F and 140 °F, the "danger zone" where bacteria multiply fast. The USDA says never thaw meat on the counter.

Three safe ways: in the fridge (safest, but plan ahead), in cold water in a leakproof bag (change the water every 30 minutes), or in the microwave (then cook it right away).

Heads up: perishable food shouldn't sit out more than 2 hours, or 1 hour at 90 °F or hotter.
