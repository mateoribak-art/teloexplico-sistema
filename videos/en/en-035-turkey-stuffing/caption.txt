Stuffed the turkey? The turkey can be done while the stuffing isn't. 🦃

The USDA says stuffing cooked inside the bird has to reach 165 °F at the center, and the only way to know is a food thermometer. The thigh can read done while the middle of the stuffing is still behind, so keep cooking the turkey until the stuffing gets there.

Heads up: for the safest, most even result, the USDA says to bake stuffing in a separate dish.
