Unplugging your phone charger every morning? It saves you pennies. 🔌

Most products that draw standby power use less than half a watt (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory). Even at half a watt all year, that's about 4 kWh, which at the average U.S. residential rate of 17.3¢/kWh (EIA, 2025, preliminary) is roughly $0.76.

But it adds up across a home: Berkeley Lab estimates standby power is 5–10% of residential electricity use. So instead of chasing one charger, put the TV setup on one power strip and switch it off, like the Energy Department suggests.

Heads up: a cable DVR or console may need power to record or update, and your savings depend on your electric rate.
