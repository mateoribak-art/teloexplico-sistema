Power's out and you keep checking the fridge? Every peek lets the cold out. 🔌

The CDC says a closed fridge keeps food safe for up to four hours. A full freezer holds about forty-eight hours (about twenty-four if it's half full), as long as the door stays closed.

Heads up: once that time is up, never taste food to find out if it's safe. And the CDC says to keep an appliance thermometer in your fridge so you can see if it stayed at forty degrees or below.

💾 Save it: door shut = up to four hours fridge, forty-eight hours full freezer.
