Cloudy day, no sunscreen? ☁️ Dermatologists say not so fast.

The American Academy of Dermatology says even on cloudy days, up to 80 percent of the sun's harmful UV rays can penetrate the clouds. You can't feel UV, and snow reflects the sun's rays too.

Heads up: clouds do block some UV, and thicker clouds block more. But the EPA says UV can still burn you on a cloudy day.

📌 Save this: SPF 30 or higher, broad-spectrum, water-resistant, reapply every two hours.
