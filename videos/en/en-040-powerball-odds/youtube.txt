Your Powerball ticket is a long shot? It's way longer than you think 🎱

The Powerball website lists the jackpot odds at 1 in 292,201,338, and the odds of winning any prize at about 1 in 24.87. Every combination has the same odds, whether it's your birthday or a quick pick.

Heads up: those are the odds for one ticket in one drawing. Ten tickets means ten chances out of 292.2 million.
