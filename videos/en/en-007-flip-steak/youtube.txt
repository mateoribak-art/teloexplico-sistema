Flip a steak only once? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Flip a steak only once? That's a myth 🥩

The idea: flip it often, and each side heats up a little, then rests a little, so the heat goes in more evenly. Food writer Harold McGee and colleagues modeled it (Physics Today, 1999). America's Test Kitchen tested it: flipping a steak every minute gave a crispier crust and more evenly cooked meat. And the juices? Poking a steak didn't drain it in a ThermoWorks test. Overcooking is what dries it out.

Heads up: this is for steaks over high heat, not burgers (America's Test Kitchen says burgers can fall apart). The two steaks in the video are an illustration, not measurements.
