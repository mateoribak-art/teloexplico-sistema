20% off + an extra 20% off is NOT 40% off 🛍️ It's 36%.

Take a $100 jacket. The first 20% off leaves $80. The extra 20% comes off the $80, not the $100: that's $16, so you pay $64.

📌 Save this: stacked discounts multiply what's left. 80% × 80% = 64% of the price, so 36% off.

Heads up: that's for deals that stack one after another. Some stores take the extra off the original price, so check the fine print.
