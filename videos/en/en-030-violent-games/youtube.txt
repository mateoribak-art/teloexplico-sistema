Do violent games make you violent? In 2020 the American Psychological Association (APA) reviewed the evidence and concluded there's insufficient scientific evidence to say violent video games cause violent behavior.

Heads up: its report did find a small but reliable link with mild aggression (like yelling or pushing), and said that's difficult to extend to more serious violence. Yelling at your screen when you lose is one thing; real violence is another.
