DESCRIPCIÓN:
🥵 The thermometer says 100° and it feels like way more? You're not exaggerating: it's your sweat.

Sweating only cools you if the sweat evaporates. In dry air it evaporates fast; in humid air, barely.

The National Weather Service worked it out for 100°F: at 15% humidity the heat index is about 96°F; at 55%, about 124°F.

Heads up: the heat index isn't measured by a thermometer, it's calculated from temperature and humidity. And those values are for shade: in full sun it can feel up to 15°F hotter.
