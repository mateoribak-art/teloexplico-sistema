On a freezing night, a slow drip can help your pipes 🚰❄️

FEMA says letting faucets drip slowly keeps water moving through the pipes, which reduces the chance of freezing. The National Weather Service adds: open the cabinet doors under your sinks so warmer air can reach the pipes, and keep the heat on, no lower than 55°F.

Heads up: this lowers the chance of freezing. It's not a guarantee.

📌 Save this: drip the faucet, open the cabinets, keep the heat on.
