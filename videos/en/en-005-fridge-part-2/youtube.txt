TÍTULO:
Cover your leftovers? #Shorts

DESCRIPCIÓN:
Cover your leftovers? Yes. But the clock matters more. 🥘

The USDA says covering food keeps moisture in and keeps it from picking up other smells in the fridge. And leftovers last 3 to 4 days in the fridge.

Won't finish them? Freeze them. For soups and stews, the USDA says quality holds 2 to 3 months in the freezer.

Heads up: all of that only counts if the food got into the fridge within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if it's above 90°F out.

Today: stick a date label on every container.
